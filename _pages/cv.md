Interests
======
I'm interested in the growing gap between what modern AI and computer vision
workloads demand and what general-purpose hardware can efficiently provide,
a gap that shows up as real, rising cost as models scale. I want to work on
closing it from both sides at once, designing algorithms, particularly in AI
and computer vision, with their eventual hardware in mind, and designing
hardware, especially FPGA and ASIC accelerators, around the structure of
the algorithms that will run on it.
